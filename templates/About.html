Our Story

Founded as a research initiative, our project focuses on combating online hate using artificial intelligence. With the rise of memes as a communication medium, we identified the urgent need for automated hate detection.

By combining deep learning models such as ResNet-50 and BERT, we created a multimodal system capable of understanding both visual and textual cues.
